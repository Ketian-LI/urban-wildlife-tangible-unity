- Animal `Feeding` maps to eating, `Resting` maps to sitting/resting, and leaving food maps to rising before movement resumes.
- The pilot intentionally reuses the existing two artwork poses with discrete transform keyframes. Once the F art direction and character designs are locked, the same frame slots can be replaced by independently drawn sprites without changing simulation logic.
